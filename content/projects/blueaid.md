# Problem

Medical bills are long, opaque and frequently wrong, and patients rarely have the time or expertise to audit them.

# How it works

An LLM pipeline extracts line items from bills and flags charges that look inconsistent, deployed on AWS.

# Results

Identified 80% of overbilling cases in evaluation; won HackMT '24.
